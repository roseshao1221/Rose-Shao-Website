In preparation · CHI 2027

Behavioral Synchrony in VR Communication

How nonverbal alignment predicts felt interpersonal connection — first author

A controlled VR study (N=44 English/Japanese dyads) comparing face-to-face and back-to-back conversation conditions, with 60+ semi-structured interviews across both language groups.

Finding: behavioral synchrony significantly predicts felt interpersonal connection (r=.31, p=.003) — direct evidence that nonverbal signals shape how connected people feel to a communication partner.
